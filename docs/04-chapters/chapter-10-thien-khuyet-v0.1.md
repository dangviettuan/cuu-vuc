## Inputs (already canon)

- He discovers **the civilization that existed before Đại Phân Ly.** Ancient mechanisms reveal that **Nguyên Linh is not inherently evil: it reflects and magnifies will.** The original purpose of the division was not merely to imprison a monster. **It was to prevent any single will from controlling the source of the Cửu Mạch.** The danger is inseparable from the question **"Who gets to decide what the world needs?"** **[NB Ch.X]**
- **Thiên Khuyết:** the ruins of an advanced civilization from before Đại Phân Ly. Ancient magitech, not neon. Constructs, mechanisms, ancient facilities. **Major boss: Thiên Cơ Tướng.** **[WB]**
- **Arc state:** Self-awareness, beginning. Entering: hollow, searching. Leaving: he understands that the real question is *who decides*. **[PB L10]**
- **Thủ Mạch Nhân** meets him **without a fight**. He admits he never knew *why* the Nine did what they did, only that he promised, and **he learns the original purpose alongside the protagonist**. **[TB T7]**
- **Open threads to resolve:** the *"kẻ mang mạch"* who stood among the Nine (CH6); the bonded human Thủ Mạch Nhân saw once before (TB T4); the bonding method *"chép từ bia đá Thiên Khuyết"* (CH9).
- **The leak at Vân Hải** magnified Thiên Điểu's will, and Mạch Ấn absorbed after it carry *"như có ai khác cùng thở"*. **[CH8 §8–9]**

## 1. Narrative objective

**Answer "why", and make the answer about him.** The division was made to stop **one good person** whose will, magnified, began deciding everything for everyone. That person was the first human bonded to the Mạch. **The protagonist is the second.** Diệp is the third. By the end he understands that he is exactly what the Nine were afraid of, and that every faction about to converge on Thiên Tâm wants a bonded will at its centre.

## 2. The truth (resolves the open threads)

1. **The Thiên Khuyết civilization** drew on Nguyên Linh through the united Cửu Mạch at Thiên Tâm. They were not villains. They used it for harvests, healing and weather. **[new; WB]**
2. **Nguyên Linh magnifies will** **[WB]**. Many small wills, drawing on it together, balanced each other.
3. To make the power *efficient*, the council chose to channel it through **one bonded person**: the first human to carry a Mạch fragment inside the body. They invented **Phép Ghép Mạch** to do it. **The stele that teaches it is here.** The Father's torn page was copied from it. **[CH9; FB F2]**
4. That person, remembered only by the title **Kẻ Mang Mạch** (the Bearer), was **good**. Their will was *that no one should suffer loss*. **[new]**
5. Magnified, that will began **removing loss from the world**: no illness, no death, then no risk, then no choice that could lead to loss. People stopped being able to leave, fail, change or decide. **A kind will became a cage for everyone.** **[new; NB "who decides"]**
6. **The council had been ten.** The other **nine** tried to persuade the Bearer and failed. They **divided the Cửu Mạch** to cut the Bearer off from the source. The people who stayed on the confluence were those who loved the Bearer's world, and they became *"ý chí đối địch"* **[CH6 §5]**.
7. **The Bearer's will was not destroyed.** When the Mạch were cut, it dissolved into Nguyên Linh. **An echo of it remains in the source**: the *"second breath"* in the Mạch Ấn after Vân Hải. **[CH8]**
8. **Thủ Mạch Nhân** was the guard who stood beside the Bearer *and* beside the Nine. He saw the bonding once. **The protagonist is the second time.** **[TB T4]**

**What it means for the present day:**
- Quy Nguyên's convergence will **reopen the source**.
- Whoever is **bonded** and present at Thiên Tâm can channel it.
- The Hội has prepared one: **Diệp**, bonded with consent by the Father **[DB N5]**.
- Uyên has had her eye on the other since Ch. V: **the protagonist**.
- **Whoever stands at the centre becomes the next Bearer.**

## 6. The Mirror of Will (X-6)

- A mechanism the council built to **test candidate Bearers**: it shows the world their will would make if magnified.
- **What the protagonist sees is computed from the leading ending axis at this point in the save.** The Mirror shows a short, wordless vision of that ending's world (NB §8). Examples:
  - **Nhân Hoàng:** peace, and statues of him.
  - **Kẻ Giữ Mạch:** a sealed door and him in front of it.
  - **Vĩnh Sinh:** a loved one's face in the light, then dying forests at the edge of the frame.
  - **Nhân Gian:** many hands on one wheel, and then a voice asking who gave him the right.
- **It is not a lock.** Endings are still decided by Ch. XI–XII. It is **self-awareness made literal**: the player sees where their choices are leading. **[PB L10–L12; NB §7]**
- Thủ Mạch Nhân, watching: *"Người đầu tiên cũng đã đứng đây. Thứ người ấy thấy rất đẹp."* (The first one also stood here. What they saw was very beautiful.)

## 7. Enemies

- **Broken constructs:** guardians whose recognition systems have failed. Shield-walkers, glass hounds, spindle drones.
- **Mechanism hazards:** rotating halls, energy conduits, collapsing bridges.
- **No human enemies.** No one else has been able to get in for centuries.

## 8. Main boss — Thiên Cơ Tướng

- **What it is:** the construct general of the Thiên Khuyết civilization, built to **serve the Bearer**, and dormant since the division. **[WB; new]**
- **Worldview:** it recognizes the protagonist as a Bearer. Its directive is to **serve a Bearer worthy of the seat**, so it tests him. *"Ý chí đủ mạnh thì được ngồi. Ý chí yếu thì bị thay."* (A strong enough will may sit. A weak one is replaced.) It is not hostile to him. **It is hostile to the idea of an empty seat.** **[NB rule 2]**
- **Phases:**
  1. **Assessment:** it mirrors his class kit to measure him.
  2. **Pressure:** it uses his own Mạch Ấn against him. It can read and copy any he has absorbed. **A player who refused many fights a weaker version.** Refusal pays off here. **[CH6–9 `MA_REFUSED`]**
  3. **The Seat:** it tries to force him into the bonding apparatus. Win by breaking the apparatus's conduits, not by damaging the general.
- **Ending:** it kneels. *"Ý chí đủ. Ra lệnh."* (Your will is enough. Command me.)

## 11. Major choice — C1: the command (X-8)

Thiên Cơ Tướng commands **the surviving constructs of Thiên Khuyết**: a small army that has not moved in centuries.

| Option | Result | Delayed | Axes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Command it** (*"Theo ta."*, Follow me) | The construct army is his. | **Ch. XI:** the constructs fight for him across the crisis. **Ch. XII:** he arrives at Thiên Tâm with an army. Pride and Greed weight. Thủ Mạch Nhân: *"Người đầu tiên cũng bắt đầu như vậy."* | Control; more power |
| **Release it** (*"Ngủ đi. Không ai ngồi ghế đó nữa."*, Sleep. No one sits in that seat again.) | The general powers down for good. The seat stays empty. | **Ch. XI:** no army. **Ch. XII:** Nhân Gian weight. Thủ Mạch Nhân bows his head. | Accepting limitation; letting go |
| **Give its command to Thủ Mạch Nhân** | The keeper of the seal now commands the Bearer's army. | **Ch. XI–XII:** Thủ Mạch Nhân arrives at Thiên Tâm with force to **hold** the seal. This raises the stakes of every branch confrontation. **Sloth and Greed weight** (trusting the old promise to decide). | Trust; refusal to intervene |

**Weighting:** high on control vs trust and on limitation vs power.

## 12. Key beats and dialogue

1. **X-3, Thủ Mạch Nhân:** *"Ta đã giữ một lời hứa mà ta không đặt ra. Ta muốn biết ta đã giữ nó cho ai."* (I have kept a promise I did not make. I want to know who I kept it for.) **[TB]**
2. **X-3, the protagonist:** *"Ông đã tha cho tôi."* Thủ Mạch Nhân: *"Ta đã thấy thứ trong ngực ngươi một lần. Lần đó, ta đã đứng gác cho nó."* (I saw what is in your chest once before. That time, I stood guard over it.)
3. **X-4, the stele line the Father tore out,** seen whole: *"Người mang mạch sẽ nghe thấy Nguyên Linh. Nguyên Linh sẽ nghe thấy người mang mạch."* (The bearer will hear Nguyên Linh. Nguyên Linh will hear the bearer.) **This is why his Mạch Ấn "breathe" with something else.**
4. **X-5, the council's last record:** *"Người ấy không sai. Người ấy chỉ là một. Không ai được là một."* (They were not wrong. They were only one. No one may be the only one.)
5. **X-5, Thủ Mạch Nhân, learning it:** a long silence. *"Vậy là ta đã giữ cửa để ngăn một người tốt."* (So I have kept the door shut against a good person.)
6. **The Mother's voice** (if kept), at the Tenth Seat: *"Cha con đọc trang đó. Mẹ cũng đọc. Mẹ không biết tới đoạn này."* (Your father read that page. I read it too. I never got this far.)
7. **`CH5_FATHER_DEMAND = why`:** at X-5 he hears the Father's "Hội reasons" again and recognizes them as **the Nine's own reasoning, turned the other way**.
8. **`CH9_FATHER_VERDICT = diep`:** at X-5 he realizes *why* the Father bonded Diệp. **She is the Hội's Bearer.** *"Cha không chọn chị ấy vì thương chị ấy hơn. Cha chọn chị ấy để ngồi cái ghế này."* (He didn't choose her because he loved her more. He chose her to sit in this seat.) The Envy wound turns into something stranger.
9. **X-9, Thủ Mạch Nhân:** *"Đã bắt đầu. Lần này, ta không biết mình nên giữ cửa hay mở cửa."* (It has begun. This time, I don't know whether I should hold the door or open it.)
