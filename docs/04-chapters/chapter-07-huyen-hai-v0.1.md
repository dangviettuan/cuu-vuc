# Chapter Spec — Chapter VII: Huyền Hải (v0.1)

Status: **v0.1 locked.** Exact tuning (encounters, boss patterns, text) is refined in production.

> Source tags: **[NB]** Narrative Bible, **[WB]** World Bible, **[PB]** Protagonist, **[FF]** Factions & Figures, **[TB]** Thủ Mạch Nhân, **[CH1–6]** chapter specs, **[new]** a new invention.

## Inputs (already canon)

- **Ancient ruins below the sea preserve records untouched by later regimes. Major reveal: the Cửu Mạch genuinely are dying.** If nothing is done, civilization eventually collapses. The story can no longer be framed as simply stopping villains from breaking a necessary seal. **[NB Ch.VII]**
- **Huyền Hải:** islands, coastal cities, tidal ruins, ancient ocean beings. Gameplay: water interaction, maritime hazards, ancient beasts. **Major boss: Huyền Kình.** **[WB]**
- **Arc state:** Conviction. Entering: seeks proof that someone was simply wrong. Leaving: the Mạch really are dying, so doing nothing is not innocent. **[PB L10]**
- **The Charter's line:** *"Bản ghi gốc được gửi xuống biển, ngoài tầm tay của chín nhà."* **[CH6 VI-8]**
- **From Ch. VI onward, absorbing a Mạch Ấn is a choice** (§9). **[CH6 §11]**
- **Cương** is a recurring rival from Ch. IV to VIII. **[FF K2]**
- He is still **alone**.

---

## 1. Narrative objective

**End the villain story for good.** He arrives hoping the original record will show that someone (the Nine, the Houses, the Hội) *broke* the world. Instead it shows that **the division itself is dying**, that the Nine knew it would, and that the Houses buried the warning. Then the chapter asks him whether *not acting* is still an option.

## 3. The original record (resolves the "why" of the decline)

The Nine sent their true record into the sea and entrusted it to **Huyền Kình**, an ancient ocean being, so that no single House could ever own it. **[new; CH6]** The record shows four things:

1. **The Nine's own measurements at the moment of division**, and **their prediction**: *"Chín mạch bị chia sẽ suy. Không thể tránh. Một nghìn năm, có thể ít hơn."* (Nine divided Mạch will decline. It cannot be avoided. A thousand years, perhaps less.)
2. **Their intent:** the division was meant to be **temporary**, *"đủ lâu để con cháu tìm ra cách khác"* (long enough for our descendants to find another way). **Why it was necessary at all is still withheld until Ch. X.**
3. **Huyền Kình's body as evidence:** its bone and coral growth layers record the Mạch's strength year by year, like tree rings. **The decline starts at the division and never stops.** It is not caused by cuts, kings or the Ty. Cuts only steepen the curve (CH4).
4. **A House seal on the archive's outer gate:** the Cửu Gia *found* the archive centuries ago, read the warning, sealed it, and left. **They knew.**

**The consequence for the story:**
- Quy Nguyên's diagnosis is **correct**.
- Their cure (convergence) remains a gamble with Nguyên Linh.
- Keeping the seal **guarantees** slow collapse.
- **Doing nothing is now a choice with a body count.** **[NB Ch.VII; WB Quy Nguyên]**

## 5. Optional areas

- **Shipwreck archive:** wrecked House vessels from earlier expeditions, with logs of earlier attempts to destroy the archive. These failed because Huyền Kình would not let them in.
- **Cảng Triều requests:** fishers whose catch is dying, and smugglers who carry Hội messages.
- **Huyền Kình's song:** at night, from the docks, its song can be heard. Codex: the port has always sung back.

## 7. Enemies

- **Sea beasts:** Linh Suy–twisted fish swarms, eels and jellyfish clouds (status hazards in the water).
- **House Lạc marines:** disciplined boarding parties with hooked spears.
- **Archive guardians:** shell-constructs on the whale's back, made by the Nine to keep out Houses (they attack anyone carrying a House seal).
- **Mini-boss: Mẫu Hà** (VII-3).

## 8. Main boss — Huyền Kình

- **Form:** an ancient whale the size of an island, its back grown over with the archive-city, and its body glowing with failing Mạch-light. **[WB; new]**
- **Worldview:** it accepted the Nine's trust and has kept the record for centuries, letting no House inside. **It is dying, and it knows it.** It does not fight to kill. **It fights to stay awake long enough for someone to read the record.** **[NB rule 2]**
- **Fight:** the arena is *inside and around its body*. The "enemy" is its **death throes**:
  - currents and pressure waves;
  - Linh Suy parasites eating it from inside;
  - collapsing archive chambers.
  - The player must reach the heart while keeping the whale conscious, by striking parasite nodes and not the whale.
- **A failure state exists, and it is not death:** if the player damages the whale itself too much, it loses consciousness early, and **part of the archive floods**, so the player reads less in VII-7.
- **Ending:** at the heart, it opens its last chamber. After the reveal it dies, slowly, through the rest of the chapter. Its Mạch Ấn rises when its song stops.

## 9. Mạch Ấn reward — Mạch Ấn Huyền Kình (optional absorption)

**System rule (from Ch. VI onward):** every Mạch Ấn absorption is a choice. The game tracks the count (`MA_REFUSED`), which weights the *accepting limitation vs more power* axis. **[CH6 §11]**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Mạch Ấn Huyền Kình — *Triều Dâng* (Rising Tide) |
| **Build effect** | Perfect dodges and parries build "tide". Releasing it sends out a **wave** that knocks enemies back and leaves them **wet**, which combines with *Liên Lôi* (Ch. IV) chain lightning. |
| **Passive** | Extended underwater breathing and the ability to dive in deep water. Opens Huyền Hải's sunken routes on revisits. |
| **If refused** | The Mạch Ấn sinks with the whale. **Lão Ngư:** *"Thì để nó về với biển."* Cảng Triều's fishers remember. |

## 10. Tide system (realm mechanic)

- The Tide Ruins surface and drown on a **tide cycle** that is visible on screen.
- Puzzles and routes depend on the tide.
- **Diving:** a breath meter, extended by air pockets and later by *Triều Dâng*.
- This is water interaction and maritime hazard made into navigation. **[WB]**

## 11. Major choice — C1: the port or the record (VII-7)

House Lạc's fleet is bombarding Cảng Triều to destroy the "Hội nest" and, with it, any hope of reaching the archive. The tide is closing over Tim Kình. He can't do everything.

| Option | Result | Delayed | Axes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Surface and defend the port** | He abandons the record with only what he has read. **The port survives.** Rạn's family lives. The record floods with the whale. | **Ch. XI:** Cảng Triều is an ally and sends ships. He knows the truth, but **cannot prove it** to anyone who wasn't there. | Action; one life vs many → many (present lives) |
| **Stay and take the record** | He secures the full original record. **Cảng Triều burns.** Rạn surfaces to find his street gone. | **Ch. XI:** the port is in ruins. **The record is proof**: in Ch. XI and XII it can be laid before the Houses, the Hội and the public (and it enables the full Nhân Gian restructuring). | Refusal to intervene (above); power through knowledge |
| **Signal Cương** (bargain with the Hội) | Cương's ships defend the port **in exchange for a copy of the record**. Both are saved. **The Hội gets the proof.** | **Ch. XI:** the port survives, and **Quy Nguyên's convergence accelerates**, with recruits and legitimacy from the proof. **His first alliance with the Hội.** Ch. VIII: Diệp has heard of it, and the duel opens on different footing. | Trust (of the "enemy") |

**Weighting:** high. **Requirement:** the option to signal Cương exists only if the VII-4 truce held (the player didn't attack Cương's divers).

## 12. Key beats and dialogue

1. **VII-1, Lão Ngư:** *"Biển không có chủ. Đó là lý do người ta giấu đồ ở đây."*
2. **VII-4, Cương:** *"Lại là ngươi, nhóc."* A pause. *"Ở Lôi Trạch, ta đã cố ngăn. Ngươi đã chặn ta."* The protagonist: *"Tôi biết."* That is the first time he admits it to anyone. **[CH4]**
3. **VII-5, the House seal:** a House sigil stamped over the Nine's warning. Rạn: *"Họ đọc rồi. Rồi họ đóng cửa lại?"*
4. **VII-7, the growth layers:** he puts his hand on the whale's bone. The light in the layers dims year by year, from the division to now. *"Không ai cắt cái này cả."* **He has been chasing culprits since the well, and the answer is that the world itself is ending.**
5. **VII-7, Cương, if signalled:** *"Ngươi biết ngươi vừa đưa cho bọn ta cái gì không?"* The protagonist: *"Biết. Và tôi biết nếu không đưa thì cả cái cảng này chết."* Cương: *"Ừ. Chào mừng đến với phép tính."*
6. **VII-9, Cương, on Diệp:** *"Cô ấy đã qua phép ghép. Cha ngươi làm. Cô ấy giờ không còn giống ngươi nhớ nữa."* **[DB N5]**

## 13. Checkpoints (ADR-0006)

- The Cảng Triều harbour shrine. It is **destroyed if the port burns**, in which case Lão Ngư's boat becomes the replacement checkpoint.
- An ancient Nine-era station in the Tide Ruins, usable only at low tide.
- An air-pocket shrine on Lưng Kình.
- No checkpoint inside Tim Kình. There is one at the whale's mouth.
