# Chapter Spec — Chapter VI: Hàn Sơn (v0.1)

Status: **v0.1 locked.** Exact tuning (encounters, boss patterns, text) is refined in production.

> Source tags: **[NB]** Narrative Bible, **[WB]** World Bible, **[PB]** Protagonist, **[FF]** Factions & Figures, **[TB]** Thủ Mạch Nhân, **[CH1–5]** chapter specs, **[new]** a new invention.

## Inputs (already canon)

- He searches **frozen battlefields** for unaltered records of the past. **Main boss: Bạch Tướng.** Reveal: **Đại Phân Ly was deliberate.** Its creators were not purely heroic: their descendants used control of the divided Mạch to build political, religious and martial power that lasted for centuries. **The same act saved humanity and enabled systemic injustice.** **[NB Ch.VI]**
- **Hàn Sơn:** a frozen mountain civilization and preserved ancient battlefields. Gameplay: frost, slow, armour, attrition. **[WB]**
- **Arc state:** Conviction. Entering: rebuilding through purpose and evidence. Leaving: even the "saviours" of history were compromised. **[PB L10]**
- **Thủ Mạch Nhân is not met in person.** The battlefields hold traces of him in the age of the Nine, and the protagonist realizes who spared him. **[TB T7]**
- **Khải's lead:** the Ty burned everything it could, but it could not burn Hàn Sơn, *"vì băng giữ chúng"*. **[CH5 §12]**
- **The Cửu Gia** claim descent from "nine founders who led the survivors". **[FF G1]**
- **He travels alone.** Diệp left in Ch. IV. **[CH4]**

---

## 1. Narrative objective

**Give him a new certainty, then compromise it.** After Ch. V he needs something solid: *the truth about the past*. Hàn Sơn gives it to him, and the truth is that the founders of his world lied, killed and divided it on purpose, and that it may have saved everyone. He also gets his first chance to **refuse power**.

## 2. Realm and locations

| Location | Function |
|---|---|
| **Đèo Gió** (Wind Pass) | The entry. Cold and attrition systems are introduced. |
| **Ưng Trại** (Eagle Hold) | The hub: a mountain hold of the **Hàn Sơn clans**, descendants of soldiers from the Đại Phân Ly war. They keep the battlefield as a sacred grave. |
| **Chiến Trường Băng** (the Ice Battlefield) | A whole army frozen mid-battle, preserved in glacier ice. Soldiers, banners and carved orders are all still legible. |
| **Hang Lệnh** (Cave of Orders) | Ice caverns holding the Nine's frozen command post: field orders carved in stone, and **the Charter**. |
| **Ngai Băng** (the Ice Throne) | The heart of the glacier, fused to Hàn Sơn's Mạch. Bạch Tướng's seat. Boss arena. |

## 3. The Hàn Sơn clans (new faction, local)

- They descend from **soldiers of the Nine's army** who stayed to guard their frozen dead. **[new]**
- Their sacred duty is to keep the battlefield undisturbed. They distrust lowlanders, the Ty and the Cửu Gia, who have sent "scholars" for centuries, and those scholars left with copies that were then burned.
- **Hàn Sơn's Mạch is failing,** and the glacier is **melting**. The clans' dead are beginning to thaw. **[WB Linh Suy]**
- **Key NPCs:**
  - **Già Ưng:** the clan chieftain, old and stern.
  - **Trúc:** about 20, a clan **ice-reader** trained to read records through the ice without breaking it. She is his guide and AI ally in VI-3 to VI-6. She is not a replacement for Diệp: she is curious, blunt and loyal to her dead, not to him.

## 5. The battlefield and the reveal

What the ice shows, in order:
1. **The other army is not an army.** They are the people who lived on the Mạch confluence around Thiên Tâm, and they **refused to leave** when the Nine announced the division. **[new]**
2. **The Nine's field orders:** *"Không ai được ở lại trên điểm hội tụ. Ai ở lại sẽ bị coi là ý chí đối địch."* (No one may remain on the confluence. Whoever remains is a hostile will.)
3. **Bạch Tướng's final order,** carved by his own hand: freeze the battlefield. Both armies. **His own soldiers were still in it.** The war ended in a single night.
4. **Thủ Mạch Nhân, young and human,** carved into the frozen tableau and named in an inscription as *"Người Giữ Mạch của Chín Vị"* (the Keeper of the Mạch for the Nine).
5. **A planted seed for Ch. X:** an inscription refers to *"kẻ mang mạch"* (the one who carries the Mạch) standing among the Nine. **[TB open question 2]**

**The Charter (Hang Lệnh):** a document drawn up *before* the war ended. It divides control of the nine Mạch among the Nine's **families**: who controls access, who licenses its use, who inherits. **The Cửu Gia's power was written down before the bodies were cold.** **[NB Ch.VI; FF G1]**

**The ambiguity, required by NB:** the same documents record why the Nine believed it necessary. A single will was already trying to reach the source. If the division had not happened, *"không còn ai để mà chia"* (there would be no one left to divide anything among). **The full reason is withheld until Ch. X.**

## 7. Enemies

- **Frost wolves:** pack hunters that inflict stacking slow.
- **Băng thi** (ice-bound): frozen soldiers animated by the leaking Mạch. **Both sides' dead rise**: Nine's soldiers in armour (heavy, armoured) and civilians (fast, desperate). Fighting the civilians is deliberately uncomfortable.
- **Avalanche and thin-ice hazards.**
- **Mini-boss: Đội Kỳ Băng** (the Frozen Standard Guard): the Nine's honour guard, still guarding a banner. **[new]**

## 8. Main boss — Bạch Tướng

- **Who:** the **White General** of the Nine's army. He gave the order to freeze the battlefield and was frozen with it. He is fused to Hàn Sơn's Mạch at the glacier's heart, half-conscious for centuries. **[new; NB]**
- **Worldview:** *"Ta đã kết thúc một cuộc chiến trong một đêm. Ta đã giết quân của chính ta để làm điều đó. Ngươi đến đây để phán xét ta? Hãy chứng minh ngươi chịu nổi sức nặng đó trước đã."* He believes some truths are graves, and that whoever opens them must be strong enough to carry what is inside. He is guilt that kept its post. **[NB rule 2]**
- **Phases:**
  1. **The General:** a disciplined armoured duelist. Frost builds on every exchange.
  2. **The Order:** he calls the frozen dead of both armies to rise around the arena. An attrition phase.
  3. **The Last Night:** he freezes the arena itself, re-enacting his final order. The player must keep moving or be entombed.
- **Ending:** he kneels. The ice around him cracks. *"Cuối cùng cũng có người đến đọc."* (At last, someone came to read it.) He dies, and his Mạch Ấn rises.

## 9. Mạch Ấn reward — Mạch Ấn Bạch Tướng (if absorbed)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Mạch Ấn Bạch Tướng — *Băng Giáp* (Ice Armour) |
| **Build effect** | Each perfect defence adds a layer of **frost armour** (damage reduction, slight slow on himself). At full layers, the armour **shatters into a frost burst** that slows all nearby enemies. This is the armour and attrition theme as a build. **[WB]** |
| **Passive** | Resistance to cold buildup. Opens frozen passages in Hàn Sơn. |

## 10. Cold system (realm mechanic)

This mirrors Ch. III's heat:
- A **cold meter** fills in exposed areas. While it is high, movement and stamina are slowed.
- **Fires, clan shrines and checkpoints** reset it.
- **Attrition:** healing items are scarcer in Hàn Sơn, so armour and positioning matter more.

## 11. Major choice — C1: refuse the Mạch Ấn (VI-7)

**For the first time, absorption is not involuntary.** In Ch. V he learned that power isn't everything. Now, as the Mạch Ấn rises, the fragment in his chest pulls, and **he can push back**. Lão Trụ's lesson ("đỡ là quyết định đòn đánh kết thúc ở đâu") becomes a choice about *where the power ends*. For non-Kiếm Khách classes the framing is their own mentor's line (CH1 §5).

| Option | Result | Delayed | Axes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Absorb** | He gains *Băng Giáp*. Without its last anchor, **the glacier starts to thaw faster.** | **Ch. XI:** the battlefield has thawed. The clans have lost their sacred ice, but **the records are open to everyone**, and the truth about the Nine spreads beyond the Houses' reach. The clans' dead are finally buried. | More power |
| **Refuse** | He turns the Mạch Ấn back into the ice. The glacier holds, for a few decades more. **He leaves without a fifth Mạch Ấn.** | **Ch. XI:** the clans keep their dead and their ice. The truth stays with him, his copies, and Trúc. Hàn Sơn becomes an ally that gives aid in Ch. XI. | Accepting limitation |

**Weighting:** high on *accepting limitation vs more power*. It is the first explicit test of the Gluttony path (PB L12).

**Mechanical honesty:** refusing must be a **real cost**. Ch. VII–VIII encounters are tuned for a player who may have either four or five Mạch Ấn, and the refusing player gets a smaller compensation: Trúc's clan gift, a frost-resistant armour piece.

## 12. Key beats and dialogue

1. **VI-3, Trúc, reading the ice:** *"Quân kia không có giáp. Họ cầm cuốc."* (Those soldiers have no armour. They are holding hoes.)
2. **VI-4, the protagonist, at the tableau:** *"Là hắn."* Trúc: *"Anh biết người này?"* He: *"Hắn tha cho tôi."*
3. **VI-5, the field order:** *"Ai ở lại sẽ bị coi là ý chí đối địch."* He reads it twice. **He thinks of Xích Vương's Wall of Names, and of Uyên's arithmetic.** For the first time he sees that the founders did the same thing, at a scale that made a world. **[CH3; CH5]**
4. **VI-5, the Charter:** Trúc, quietly: *"Họ chia nhau thế giới trước khi chôn người chết."* (They divided the world among themselves before they buried their dead.)
5. **VI-6, Bạch Tướng (see §8).**
6. **VI-7, if refused:** Trúc: *"Lần đầu tiên tôi thấy có người không lấy."* (It's the first time I've seen anyone not take it.)
7. **VI-8, Già Ưng:** *"Chúng ta luôn biết. Người ta canh mộ không phải vì quên. Mà vì nhớ quá rõ."* (We always knew. People don't guard a grave because they forgot. They guard it because they remember too clearly.)

## 13. Checkpoints (ADR-0006)

- Clan shrines (warming fires) at Ưng Trại and in the pass.
- **An ancient Nine-era Mạch station** frozen in the battlefield. Its reignition is optional, and its design is a direct ancestor of the Người Dẫn Mạch stations. **[TB T5 motif]**
- No checkpoint inside the Ice Throne. One sits at the glacier gate.
