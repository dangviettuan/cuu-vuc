# Factions & Key Figures v0.1 — Quy Nguyên / Trung Vực and beyond

Status: **v0.1 complete.** The open questions below are still pending.

Scope: the first pass covers four factions and five key figures. The four class mentors are defined in the Chapter I spec (`docs/04-chapters/chapter-01-thanh-moc-v0.1.md` §5).

## Canon locked (from existing bibles)

- **Quy Nguyên Hội:** a secret, multi-generational organization that researches Linh Suy. It concludes that the current system guarantees collapse and seeks to reconnect the Cửu Mạch, which would break the seal. It is not the canonical villain. **[WB]**
- **Trung Vực / Thiên Kinh:** politics, religion, trade, schools, factional power. Prosperous at first, then unstable, then a centre of systemic crisis. **[WB, NB Ch.II, V]**
- **The Nine's descendants** turned control of the divided Mạch into political, religious and martial power **[NB Ch.VI]**.
- **Xích Vương:** the ruler of Xích Sa. He diverts Linh resources from small settlements to keep the central city alive. He may become a boss or stay an ally. **[NB Ch.III]**
- **The Father** trained in Thiên Kinh under the institution that licenses Người Dẫn Mạch. In Ch. II the protagonist finds his old records, **a former teacher** and his reputation. **[FB F1]**
- **Diệp** apprenticed with the Mộc Thành forest wardens. **[DB N1]**
- **Làng Khê:** Diệp's home settlement, destroyed by the Father's procedure. Its stream ran dry first, the way the An Mộc well does in Chương 1. **[FB F2, DB N1]**
- In Ch. I the protagonist meets the four class mentors at Mộc Thành. **[PB L9]**

## Part A — Factions

### G1. Cửu Gia — the Nine Houses of Thiên Kinh

- These are the ruling noble houses of Trung Vực. Each claims descent from one of the **nine founders who led humanity's survivors** through Đại Phân Ly. **[new; NB Ch.VI]**
- **Public story:** heroes who saved people from a catastrophe.
- **Ch. VI truth:** they descend from the Nine who *caused* it deliberately, and each house's power rests on controlling access to one Mạch.
- They rule Thiên Kinh through a council. Rivalries between the houses drive the political chaos of Ch. V. **[new]**

### G2. Thiên Mạch Ty — the Mạch Bureau

- The Thiên Kinh institution that **trains and licenses every Người Dẫn Mạch** and controls Mạch maintenance and access across the realms. It answers to the Cửu Gia. **[new; resolves FB open question]**
- It teaches the public history: Đại Phân Ly was a catastrophe, and Linh Suy is local, seasonal and manageable. **[new]**
- It keeps restricted archives. The Father learned the forbidden bonding method from them **[FB F2]**.
- **Ch. II:** it suppresses the protagonist's evidence. **Ch. IV:** it leads the well-intended restoration in Lôi Trạch that causes mass casualties. **[new; NB Ch.II, IV]**
- **Hidden hypocrisy:** Ty **seal-emblems are made from Mạch fragments**. Its enforcers' power is carved from the system it forbids anyone to cut. Ty forge alloy has leaked into Quy Nguyên's cutting tools for decades. **[Ch. II spec]**

### G3. Hộ Lâm — the Mộc Thành wardens

- A local order of forest wardens that protects Thanh Mộc's trails, villages and Mạch stations alongside the Người Dẫn Mạch. **[new; resolves DB open question]**
- **Loyal to the forest, not to Thiên Kinh.** They distrust the Ty, which treats Thanh Mộc as a resource, and they turn hostile to Quy Nguyên after the Thanh Mộc cut. **[new]**
- Diệp trained with them for nine years. Her move to Quy Nguyên is felt as a desertion. **[DB]**

### G4. Quy Nguyên Hội — internal structure

- Small, independent **cells** led by a single **Hội Chủ**. Researchers (like the Father) and field operatives (like Cương, K2) rarely know one another's full plans. **[new]**
- **Internal fault line:** *patience* (the Hội Chủ: prove it, prepare it, then converge) against *urgency* (Cương: every season of delay kills more people than any cut). Diệp rises between the two camps. **[new; DB N5]**
- This structure explains why the Father was told the cut would happen somewhere other than near An Mộc **[FB F4]**.

## Part B — Key figures

### K1. Hội Chủ Uyên — leader of Quy Nguyên Hội

- **Female, about 70.** She was a master archivist of the Thiên Mạch Ty and **the Father's teacher in Thiên Kinh**. **[new; FB F1 "former teacher"]**
- **Defection:** decades ago she found Ty records of Mạch decline that had been suppressed for generations, and she left to lead Quy Nguyên. After the procedure she recruited her former student. **[new; FB F4]**
- **Worldview:** *"The seal is a promise made by the dead, on behalf of the unborn, without asking either."* She sincerely protects the future generations who will inherit the collapse. **[new]**
- **Flaw:** she treats generations as abstractions and spends present lives patiently. She has never seen a cut take effect in person. **[new]**
- **Link to the protagonist:** she knows the Father's procedure worked, but not that the child survived (FB F4, his "last lie"). When the protagonist starts absorbing Mạch Ấn, she works it out and takes a deep interest in him. **[new]**
- **Arc:** a voice and rumour in Ch. II, met in person in Ch. V, leads the convergence at Thiên Tâm in Ch. XI–XII. **[new; NB]**

### K2. Cương — Quy Nguyên's Đoạn Mạch Sứ (the one who cuts)

- **Male, about 40.** The field commander of the Hội's cutting operations. **He ordered the Thanh Mộc cut, earlier and closer to An Mộc than the Father was told.** **[new; FB F4]**
- **Background:** a former Thiên Mạch Ty enforcer who suppressed Linh Suy outbreak reports for years, until one of them was his own home region. He once silenced the truth for the old system; now he cuts the Mạch for the new one. **[new]**
- **Worldview:** *"Time is the one resource no one can make. Every season of delay kills more than any cut."* **[new]**
- **Story role:**
  - **End of Ch. I:** the first human face of Quy Nguyên. The protagonist finds Cương's cell at the cut site after Mộc Giác. He becomes the obvious "villain" that confirms the protagonist's certainty **[PB L10]**, and he walks away without a fight. **[new]**
  - **Ch. IV–VIII:** a recurring rival. Diệp's rise inside the Hội puts the two of them in tension. **[new]**
  - His fate depends on the branch; it is defined in the chapter specs.

### K3. Chưởng Ty Khải — head of the Thiên Mạch Ty

- **Male, mid-fifties.** He was **the Father's classmate and rival** under Uyên. He stayed and rose to lead the Ty. **[new]**
- **One classroom, three paths:** the teacher who left (Uyên), the student who left (the Father), and the student who stayed (Khải). **[new]**
- **Worldview:** *"The system is rotten, but it is the only thing feeding nine realms. Panic kills faster than Linh Suy."* He believes in reform from inside and serves the Cửu Gia while knowing their rot. **[new]**
- **Story role:**
  - **Ch. II:** knew the Father and is warm toward the son. He suppresses the protagonist's evidence. This is the first institutional betrayal. **[new; NB Ch.II]**
  - **Ch. IV:** leads the Lôi Trạch restoration, which kills many people. He is a sincere man whose good intentions end in a massacre. **[new; NB Ch.IV]**
  - **Ch. V onward:** the Cửu Gia make him their scapegoat in the chaos. His fate depends on the branch.

### K4. Xích Vương Hạo — ruler of Xích Sa

- **Male, about 45.** He rules from the central city of **Xích Thành**. **[new; NB Ch.III]**
- **His decision:** he diverts Mạch-well water from the peripheral settlements to keep Xích Thành alive. Thousands survive, and others are knowingly abandoned. **[NB]**
- **What keeps him from being a villain:** the **Tường Tên** (Wall of Names). He personally carves the name of every settlement he abandons into the palace wall and visits each one before its well is cut off. He refuses to forget the people he chose not to save. **[new]**
- **Worldview:** *"A king who tries to save everyone saves no one."* **[new]**
- **The mirror:** the Father saved one life at the cost of many; Xích Vương saves many at the cost of a few. This is the "one life vs many" axis in its purest form. **[new; NB §7]**
- **Ch. III choice (guidance):** the player can enforce the diversion, break it so that everyone shares the risk and the city may fall, or overthrow him. He becomes a boss or remains an ally depending on that choice. **[NB]**

### K5. Lâm Trưởng Ngàn — commander of the Hộ Lâm

- **Female, about 50.** Commander of the Mộc Thành wardens and **Diệp's mentor**. **[new]**
- **Worldview:** local loyalty. *"Thiên Kinh is far away. Quy Nguyên is an idea. The forest is right here."* She distrusts both sides equally. **[new]**
- **Story role:**
  - **Ch. I:** the hub authority at Mộc Thành. She helps the protagonist and gives him access to the class mentors (with Diệp, DB N6).
  - **Ch. IV–V:** Diệp's departure is a personal wound for her.
  - **Later revisits to Thanh Mộc:** she shows the consequences of the cut and of the player's choices for the forest. **[new]**
