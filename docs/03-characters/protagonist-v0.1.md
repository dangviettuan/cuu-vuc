# Protagonist Bible v0.1

Status: **In progress**

## Canon locked

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 25 at the beginning of the game
- **Name:** Player-defined at New Game via text input. There is no canonical protagonist name.
- **Visual customization:** None. The protagonist has a fixed authored appearance. The player does not customize face, hair, body type, skin tone, or other cosmetic identity traits.
- **Onboarding rule:** Character setup should be minimal. The player enters a name and proceeds directly into the game experience without a character-creator flow.
- **Role:** Authored protagonist, not a blank avatar.
- **Origin:** Làng An Mộc, Thanh Mộc Vực.
- **Family anchor:** Son of a Người Dẫn Mạch.
- **Narrative function:** A human being who gradually gains enough power to affect the fate of Cửu Vực, then must confront whether power grants the moral right to choose for others.
- **Initial worldview:** There is a right choice and a wrong choice; good people protect others and bad people harm the innocent.
- **Core arc:** certainty → doubt → conviction → collapse → self-awareness.
- **Hidden origin:** As a child, he survived Linh Suy because his father used a forbidden method to implant a fragment of Linh Mạch into him. His survival came at a cost to other people.
- **Gameplay identity:** Class is chosen by the player and represents the path of combat he learns, not a change of protagonist identity.

## Design rule

The protagonist must remain recognizable as the same person across every branch. Player choices reveal, strengthen, distort, or challenge traits that already exist in him; endings must not feel like sudden personality swaps.

The fixed authored appearance is intentional: Cửu Vực is an offline story-driven action RPG, so visual identity supports stronger art direction, animation consistency, cutscene staging, narrative recognition, and faster access to gameplay. Player individuality should come primarily from name, class, attributes, skills, equipment, Mạch Ấn, decisions, relationships, and ending path rather than cosmetic character creation.
