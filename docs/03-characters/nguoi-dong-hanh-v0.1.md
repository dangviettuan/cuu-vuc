# Người Đồng Hành Bible v0.1 — Diệp

Status: **v0.1 complete.** The open questions below are still pending.

Role name: **Người Đồng Hành** (the Companion). **Personal name (locked): Diệp**, meaning *leaf*. It ties her to Thanh Mộc and to the yellow leaf in Chương 1.

## Canon locked

- **Identity:** an orphan **survivor ward**. Her home, **Làng Khê**, a small forest settlement in Thanh Mộc, died because of the Father's forbidden procedure **[Father bible F2]**. The Father took her in out of guilt and raised her with his son in An Mộc.
- **Gender:** Female.
- **Age:** 27 at the start of the game, two years older than the protagonist.
- **Bond:** sibling-like. **No romance**, now or later.
- **Prologue:** away at Mộc Thành when the well dries. She and the protagonist are reunited in Chapter I. **[PB]**
- **From the Protagonist Bible (L8):** she is the one person whose judgment he takes seriously, and the one who says the hard thing. People trust, love and forgive her more easily. He notices this and laughs it off. This is the seed of the Envy path.
- **From the Narrative Bible:**
  - They fight together at first.
  - Her belief: *"Refusing to sacrifice some people can become a decision to let everyone die."*
  - She openly sympathizes with Quy Nguyên in Ch. IV, and the relationship fractures there.
  - By Ch. VIII she is a major Quy Nguyên figure, and they fight an ideological duel in Vân Hải.
  - She is ally, rival, emotional anchor and philosophical mirror.
  - She has a central role in the Kẻ Thay Thế ending.

## Canon locked — detail

> Approved from proposals N1–N9. **N8–N9 are locked as design guidance**: binding direction, with exact beats refined in chapter specs. Source tags: **[NB]** Narrative Bible, **[PB]** Protagonist Bible, **[FB]** Father Bible, **[GB]** Gameplay Bible, **[Ch1]** prose draft, **[new]** a new invention.

### N1. Backstory timeline

| Years before game | Event |
|---|---|
| ~20 | She is 7 when the Father cuts the fragment from the node next to her settlement. Over the following season the land dies and her family dies with it. She is found among the last survivors. **[FB F2]** |
| ~20 | The Father brings her to An Mộc. The village is told her settlement fell to early Linh Suy, the same sickness the Father's son "miraculously" survived. **[new]** |
| ~20–9 | She grows up in the Father's house with the protagonist. The two "survivors of Linh Suy" bond as siblings. She protects him and he follows her into the forest. **[new]** |
| ~9 | At 18 she leaves for **Mộc Thành** to apprentice with the **Hộ Lâm**, the forest wardens of Thanh Mộc, under Lâm Trưởng Ngàn. From then on the household is two people. **[new; Ch1 "hai cha con"]** |
| 0 | She is a respected warden at Mộc Thành, 27 years old. **[new]** |

- She **remembers** her settlement: the smell of its water, her mother's voice, the empty houses. She has always believed Linh Suy took it. **[new]**
- She does **not** know about the procedure, the split fragment or the hidden half Mạch Ấn until N4.

### N2. Personality

- **Steady and warm.** She is easy with people and people relax around her. This is why she is trusted and forgiven more easily (L8). **[PB]**
- **Plainly honest.** She says the hard thing gently but does not soften its content. **[NB, PB]**
- **Pragmatic about loss.** She learned at 7 that Linh Suy takes people whether or not anyone decides. This is the root of her later belief. **[new; NB §4]**
- **Protective, like an older sister.** She taught the protagonist the forest trails and still treats him as someone to look after. He finds this both comforting and infuriating. **[new]**
- **Grief kept private.** She rarely talks about her settlement, and never in self-pity. **[new]**

### N3. Flaw, fear, wound (designed to mirror the protagonist)

| | Protagonist (L4–L6) | Người Đồng Hành |
|---|---|---|
| **Flaw** | Cannot tolerate uncertainty, so he acts too soon | **Accepts costs too readily**, including costs to herself. She treats people, herself included, as quantities in a sum. |
| **Fear** | Being helpless while loved ones are in danger | **Being a burden**: being kept alive at someone else's expense *again*. |
| **Wound** | Protected but never trusted by the Father | **Trusted but never sure she was loved.** She suspects the Father took her in out of duty, not love. |

The mirror is the point. He envies her for being trusted. She envies him for being the Father's real son. Only his envy can become an ending. **[new; NB ending IV]**

### N4. Learning the truth before him

- **Ch. IV:** her sympathy for Quy Nguyên starts from ideology, not revenge. The well-intended restoration in Lôi Trạch kills people too, which convinces her that preserving the old system is not innocent. **[NB Ch.IV]**
- **Between Ch. IV and V:** after the fracture, she seeks out the Father. **He tells her the truth.** He believes the victim has more right to it than the person it saved. **[new; FB F5]**
- **Her response:** she does not excuse him, and she does not hate him either. *He chose, and he carries it. Linh Suy never chose and never carried anything.* The truth deepens a conviction she already had. It does not create it. **[new]**
- **She keeps the secret from the protagonist.** She believes it would break him. Without noticing, she becomes like the Father: protecting someone by withholding the truth. **[new; PB L6]**
- **Ch. IX:** when the protagonist learns the truth in U Minh, he also learns that **she knew**, and that the Father trusted her with what he never told his son. This is the sharpest point of the Envy seed. **[new; NB Ch.IX, PB L12]**

### N5. Role in Quy Nguyên Hội

- She joins after learning the truth, **voluntarily** and for her own reasons. She is not following the Father. **[new]**
- She rises quickly, because people trust her (N2). By Ch. VIII she leads Quy Nguyên operations in Vân Hải. **[new; NB Ch.VIII]**
- **The consented procedure:** she volunteers for a refined version of the Father's method, a deliberate, consented bonding of a Mạch fragment. She becomes on purpose what the protagonist became as a dying child. The Father performs it.
  - This gives the Ch. VIII duel mechanical credibility.
  - It sharpens the theme: consent against necessity.
  - It feeds the Envy wound: the Father chose *her* to carry his work forward. **[new]**

### N6. Gameplay and combat identity

- **Ch. I–IV ally:** she fights beside the protagonist as a story-driven NPC ally, AI-controlled in scripted segments, not player-controlled. **[new; NB "at first they fight together"]**
- **Ch. I:** she brings him to the four mentors at Mộc Thành and makes the introductions. This ties into L9. **[new; PB L9]**
- **Weapon:** a **warden's spear**. Its long reach and sweeping, controlled style contrast with every player class and with the brush-knife he starts with. **[new]**
- **Ch. VIII duel:** a mechanically credible boss fight. Her warden spear technique is amplified by the bonded fragment (N5). Some patterns echo moves she used as an ally, so the player recognizes them. **[new]**

### N7. Visual identity

- A little taller than the protagonist. Athletic, with an upright and composed stance. **[new]**
- **Warden kit:** a green-grey layered coat, wrapped forearms and the spear across her back. **[new]**
- **Silhouette anchor:** a small wooden charm on a cord, the only object she kept from her settlement. It is never removed in any branch. **[new]**
- **After joining Quy Nguyên:** the warden coat gives way to Hội attire. She keeps the charm. After N5, a visible bonding mark appears on her body, a deliberate echo of his hidden chest scar. **[new]**

### N8. Relationship state after the Ch. VIII duel (design guidance)

The duel resolves into one of these persistent states, which Ch. XI–XII consume:

| State | How it is reached (examples) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Đồng Hành** | Cooperate | Allied despite disagreement. She may fight beside him again in Ch. XI. |
| **Tôn Trọng** | Refuse her, but spare her | Apart, with respect intact. She can help or hinder in Ch. XI depending on his choices. |
| **Đoạn Tuyệt** | Fight, then deliberately sever | The bond is broken. She becomes an opponent in Ch. XI. |
| **Tử** | Fight, and kill her | She is dead. She appears only in U Minh (Ch. IX revisit) and in the Vĩnh Sinh path. |

### N9. Fates by ending (design guidance)

| Ending | Her fate |
|---|---|
| Nhân Hoàng (Pride) | She refuses to live under one will, and leaves or resists. He must decide whether to overrule her too. |
| Kẻ Giữ Mạch (Greed) | She is shut out from Nguyên Linh "for safety", the way he was shut out from the truth. |
| Ngày Không Còn Thần (Wrath) | She accepts the cost of destroying the system. It is the one ending where they agree, too late. |
| **Kẻ Thay Thế (Envy)** | He defeats her and takes her place in the Hội, finishing the path she started. **[NB]** |
| Vĩnh Sinh (Desire) | If she died (state Tử), she is the candidate for resurrection, and her own philosophy condemns that choice. The Father is the alternative candidate. **[FB F8]** |
| Vạn Mạch Chi Thân (Gluttony) | The bonded fragment in her is among the power he consumes. |
| Thế Giới Cũ (Sloth) | She stays in the world he refused to change and keeps fighting Linh Suy without him. |
| Nhân Gian | She becomes one of the people the new order distributes power to. She is the first to ask whether the new order is truly his to impose. Thủ Mạch Nhân then asks the final question **[TB T8]**. **[NB]** |
