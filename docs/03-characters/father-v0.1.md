# Father Bible v0.1

Status: **v0.1 complete.** The open questions below are still pending.

Working reference: **the Father**. **Name rule (locked):** no personal name for now. The son calls him "cha", and An Mộc addresses him by title ("thầy Dẫn Mạch" / "ông Dẫn Mạch"). A name may be revealed later, for example through Thiên Kinh records.

## Canon locked

These points are already canon elsewhere. They are collected here and not re-decided.

- **Profession:** Người Dẫn Mạch: inspects and maintains infrastructure connected to the Linh Mạch. **[NB §2]**
- **Home:** Làng An Mộc, Thanh Mộc Vực. He and the protagonist live in a two-person household. **[Protagonist bible]**
- **Forbidden procedure:** When the protagonist was a child dying of Linh Suy, the Father implanted a fragment of Linh Mạch into him. It worked. Obtaining and using the fragment caused an incident in which other people died or suffered lasting harm. **[NB §3]**
- **Mother:** She died in, or as a direct consequence of, that incident. The exact mechanism belongs to this bible. **[Protagonist bible]**
- **Quy Nguyên Hội:** He is an important member. He left An Mộc **voluntarily**. He was not abducted or coerced. **[NB §5]**
- **Core belief:** Linh Suy cannot be solved by preserving the current world structure. Refusing responsibility is worse than carrying guilt. He does not believe he is morally pure. **[NB §5]**
- **Ward:** He raised Diệp (Người Đồng Hành), an orphan of the settlement his procedure destroyed. Between Ch. IV and V he tells her the truth, and he performs her consented bonding procedure. See [`nguoi-dong-hanh-v0.1.md`](nguoi-dong-hanh-v0.1.md).
- **Story beats:** He disappears in the Prologue. The protagonist finds him in Ch. V, and neither can persuade the other. Ch. IX reveals the procedure. In Ch. XI he must commit to what he believes. **[NB]**

## Canon locked — detail

> Approved from proposals F1–F8. **F7–F8 are locked as design guidance**: binding direction, with exact beats refined in chapter specs. Source tags: **[NB]** Narrative Bible, **[WB]** World Bible, **[PB]** Protagonist Bible, **[Ch1]** prose draft, **[new]** a new invention.

### F1. Background and training

- He was born in An Mộc. As a young man he was sent to **Thiên Kinh** to train as a Người Dẫn Mạch under the institution that licenses Mạch maintenance. The institution's name is TBD. It descends from the power structures built on the divided Mạch. **[new; WB, NB Ch.VI]**
- He was a gifted, idealistic student, as certain of right and wrong as his son is now. He returned to An Mộc, married, and served as the district's Người Dẫn Mạch. **[new]**
- **Story use:** in Ch. II the protagonist reaches Thiên Kinh and finds traces of the Father's past: old records, a former teacher, a reputation. These are the first hints that the Father knew far more than a village maintainer should. **[new; NB Ch.II]**

### F2. The forbidden procedure (≈20 years before the game)

- **Age:** The protagonist was about five. The Father was in his early thirties. **[new; consistent with Ch1: father "ngoài năm mươi", son 25]**
- **Method:** Knowledge he had learned in Thiên Kinh and was never meant to use: how to cut a fragment from a living Linh Mạch node and bond it to a living body. **[new]**
- **Where:** A Mạch node deep in Thanh Mộc, beside a small forest settlement. The settlement's name is TBD. **[new]**
- **What happened:**
  1. Cutting the fragment destabilized the node. Over the following season the settlement's land died, its people sickened, and it was abandoned. The public record calls it an early local Linh Suy collapse. **[new; NB §3 "other people died"]**
  2. The **Mother** came with them and held the child during the bonding. The backlash passed through her, and she died. **She knew the risk to herself and chose it. The settlement never got that choice.** **[new; PB mother lock]**
  3. The backlash burned the Father's **left hand** from wrist to palm. **[Ch1: white burn scar]**
  4. The fragment entered the child's **chest**. **[Ch1: chest scar]**
- **Cover story in An Mộc:** The Mother died of the same sickness that the child miraculously survived. Only the Father knows the full truth. The shrine keeper may suspect it; see F7.

### F3. The burned half Mạch Ấn (resolves the "two Mạch Ấn" question)

- The fragment was too large to bond safely, so the Father **split it**. Half went into the child. The other half was burned out by the backlash and became the blackened, half-broken object hidden under the floorboard. **[new; Ch1]**
- It is still bound to the half inside the protagonist, which is why it pulses with his heartbeat. **[Ch1: "Nhịp thứ ba trùng chính xác với nhịp tim của anh"]**
- **Why it was hidden, not destroyed:** the Father feared that destroying it might harm his son. It is evidence of his crime, and it is also the one thing he could not throw away. He never meant his son to find it. **[new]**
- **Why it wakes now:** the Thanh Mộc Mạch was cut that day (NB Ch.I), and the disturbance reaches the fragment in both halves. **[new]**
- **Relation to the first Mạch Ấn (Mộc Giác):** the half is **not absorbed**. It is a *resonator*, not a power-up.
  - **Prologue/Ch. I gameplay:** it lets him pass the regrowing roots that block the path to the station **[Ch1]**. The forest recognizes Mạch it lost.
  - **Mộc Giác** guards the Mạch the fragment was cut from. In the fight it recognizes what is inside him. This adds unease to the protagonist's first boss victory, not triumph. **[new; NB Ch.I]**
  - The first true absorption happens after Mộc Giác and is possible only because of the implanted half **[NB §3]**. At that moment the burned half crumbles, its function spent. **[new]**

### F4. Role in Quy Nguyên Hội

- **How he joined:** About two years after the procedure, Quy Nguyên contacted him. His procedure had proven that a Mạch fragment could be cut, moved and bonded to a human. That is evidence that the Cửu Mạch can be *rejoined*. He joined believing in the cause. **[new; WB Quy Nguyên]**
- **Rank:** a long-serving field researcher, senior and respected, the Hội's leading authority on how Mạch behaves when cut and rejoined. **[new; NB "important member"]**
- **His cover:** He stayed in An Mộc for eighteen years as an ordinary Người Dẫn Mạch. His station measurements (**[Ch1]** the notebook of numbers, the old map with Mạch lines) fed the Hội's research. **[new]**
- **The cut on the day the game begins:**
  - **He did not cut it himself.** But his measurements are why Thanh Mộc was chosen as the site, and he knew a cut was coming. **[new; NB Ch.I]**
  - At the well, the third rod's vibration tells him it is happening *now*, closer to An Mộc than he was told. **[Ch1: the rod, the tell, "Không."]**
  - He goes to the station to confirm, then leaves with the Hội instead of coming home. That is the voluntary departure. **[NB §5]**
- **His last lie:** He kept his son out of the Hội's records. They know his procedure succeeded, but not that the child survived into adulthood. When the protagonist starts absorbing Mạch Ấn, that protection breaks. **[new]**

### F5. Personality and voice

- **Few words.** His answers close a subject rather than open it. **[Ch1: "Có thứ hiểu thì tốt. Có thứ hiểu sớm lại không tốt."]**
- **Dry deflection,** the source of his son's humour. **[Ch1: "Cha biết. Cha có mặt hôm con sinh."]**
- **A skilled liar with one tell:** his right thumb rubs the first joint of his index finger. He does not know he has it. **[Ch1]**
- **Neat, methodical, patient:** the opposite of his son's restlessness. **[Ch1: the room "ngăn nắp đến khó chịu"]**
- **Love shown as protection, never as explanation.** He physically stops himself from reaching out. **[Ch1: the hand on the shoulder; PB L6]**
- **He never asks for forgiveness.** He believes asking would shift the weight of his choice onto the person he wronged. **[new; NB §5]**
- **His mirror:** He sees his younger, certain self in his son. That is why he will not argue his son out of certainty: he knows only experience can do that. **[new]**

### F6. Visual identity

- In his fifties and looks older: sun-darkened skin, fine lines at the eyes, a slight stoop from years of kneeling at stations. **[Ch1]**
- **Fixed marks:** the white burn scar on his left hand **[Ch1]**, and the right-thumb tell as an animation beat in cutscenes.
- **Prologue kit:**
  - a worn leather Người Dẫn Mạch bag with a grey, frayed strap;
  - three forearm-length copper rods engraved with leaf-vein lines;
  - a spool of **red thread** (used to mark measured Mạch lines; its loop on the son's wrist is a deliberate visual echo);
  - a small locked wooden box. **[Ch1; PB L1]**
- **After Ch. V:** Quy Nguyên attire. He keeps the burn uncovered and no longer carries the Dẫn Mạch bag. Its absence is the visual sign that he has stopped maintaining the old system. **[new]**
- **The locked box:** contents TBD. Reserve it for a later reveal (Ch. IX or XI). **[Ch1]**

### F7. Relationship evolution (Father's side)

| Phase | The Father's position |
|---|---|
| Prologue | Protects his son by withholding everything. He knows the day he dreaded has come. |
| Ch. I–IV | Absent. He leaves traces. He may intervene indirectly, e.g. ensuring a Hội cell does not harm his son, without revealing himself. **[new]** |
| Ch. V | Meets his son openly. Does not defend himself, does not ask him to join, does not reveal the procedure. **[NB]** |
| Ch. IX | The truth comes out through U Minh, not through his confession. Whether he was ever going to tell is left ambiguous. **[NB]** |
| Ch. XI–XII | Commits fully to Quy Nguyên's convergence at Thiên Tâm. His final stance toward his son depends on branch state. |

**The shrine keeper** (Ch1) knew the Mother and may suspect the cover story. They are a possible source of the first hints, in the Prologue or on a revisit. **[new]**

### F8. Combat role and possible fates

- **No mandatory boss fight.** The relationship is resolved through choices, not his health bar. He may become a branch-only confrontation in Ch. XI or XII. **[new; NB narrative rules 1–3]**
- **Possible fates (design guidance, per ending):**

| Ending | The Father's fate |
|---|---|
| Nhân Hoàng (Pride) | Lives under the protagonist's order and is spared, which he experiences as being overruled. The son now decides for everyone, as he once decided for a settlement. |
| Kẻ Giữ Mạch (Greed) | Imprisoned or kept away from Nguyên Linh "for safety". The son hides the dangerous thing, as the Father once did. |
| Ngày Không Còn Thần (Wrath) | Dies in the destruction of the system, possibly by the protagonist's hand in a branch confrontation. |
| Kẻ Thay Thế (Envy) | Chooses Người Đồng Hành's path over his son's, confirming the protagonist's wound. |
| Vĩnh Sinh (Desire) | A candidate for the person the protagonist cannot let go of (the alternative is Người Đồng Hành), or the one who warns him what resurrection costs, having paid that price once. |
| Vạn Mạch Chi Thân (Gluttony) | Recognizes that the fragment he implanted has become the hunger consuming the world. His original choice comes full circle. |
| Thế Giới Cũ (Sloth) | Both men return to An Mộc and live in the same house, in silence. |
| Nhân Gian | Faces judgment under the new restructured order and accepts it, without asking forgiveness. |
