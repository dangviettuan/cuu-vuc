# Thủ Mạch Nhân Bible v0.1

Status: **v0.1 complete.** The open questions below are still pending.

## Canon locked

- **Origin:** a **sworn guardian** of the Nine who divided the Cửu Mạch during Đại Phân Ly. He is **not** one of the Nine. He is bound to the seal they created. Core line: *"Ta giữ một lời hứa mà ta không hề đặt ra."* (I keep a promise I never made.)
- **Gender:** Male.
- **From the Narrative Bible (Ch. V):**
  - He is a surviving figure from the Đại Phân Ly era.
  - The boss fight against him is real and mechanically credible. Phase 1 can be beaten.
  - After the apparent victory he stops holding back. The normal boss health structure disappears, and the tools the player relies on are systematically countered.
  - The player is defeated without a Game Over, and the story continues.
  - The fight communicates two things: (1) no level of human power is necessarily absolute; (2) combat strength does not grant moral authority over the world's future.
- **From the Narrative Bible rules:** like every major boss, he has a worldview and something he sincerely protects.

## Canon locked — detail

> Approved from proposals T1–T8. **T7–T8 are locked as design guidance**: binding direction, with exact beats refined in chapter specs. Source tags: **[NB]** Narrative Bible, **[WB]** World Bible, **[PB]** Protagonist Bible, **[FB]** Father Bible, **[DB]** Diệp Bible, **[GB]** Gameplay Bible, **[new]** a new invention.

### T1. Origin and survival

- He was a human warrior, the Nine's most trusted guard. When the Cửu Mạch were divided, the Nine bound him **into the seal itself** as its living keeper: the one will left to watch over the arrangement after they were gone. **[new; WB]**
- **His life runs on the divided Mạch.** He does not age while the seal holds. **Linh Suy is killing him too**: his body is cracking as the Mạch weaken. The one being who cannot be beaten is, quietly, dying. **[new; WB Linh Suy]**
- **He has forgotten his own name.** Thủ Mạch Nhân is a title, and he has answered to nothing else for centuries. This fits the project's theme of names: the player-named protagonist, the title-only Father. **[new]**
- He knew the Nine only as their guard. He **did not** take part in their deliberations, so he cannot answer the questions saved for Ch. VI and Ch. X. **[new; protects NB reveals]**

### T2. Philosophy

- **He serves the seal, not justice.** For centuries he watched the Nine's descendants turn control of the divided Mạch into political, religious and martial power **[NB Ch.VI]**, and he did nothing, because his oath covered the seal and not the people living under it.
- **His core belief:** *no one alive is fit to decide what replaces the seal, including himself.* So he keeps it, even knowing it is failing. **[new]**
- **His moral weight:** his loyalty is real, and his non-intervention enabled centuries of injustice. He is the living form of Thế Giới Cũ (Sloth) and of Kẻ Giữ Mạch (Greed) as guardianship: a mirror of two of the protagonist's possible endings. **[new; NB endings]**
- **He opposes Quy Nguyên** because they would break the seal. He is **not** an ally of the Trung Vực institutions that exploit it. **[new]**
- **Voice:** sparse, archaic, patient. He addresses everyone as "ngươi" and never raises his voice. **[new]**

### T3. The Ch. V encounter (scene)

- In the chaos of Thiên Kinh, the protagonist has just found the Father and learned that he joined Quy Nguyên voluntarily **[NB Ch.V; FB F7]**.
- Thủ Mạch Nhân arrives to **kill the Father**, the man whose work proved the Mạch can be cut and rejoined **[FB F4]**.
- **The protagonist steps between them,** minutes after feeling betrayed. His protectiveness and his fear of helplessness override his judgment **[PB L3, L5]**.
- **He loses. The Father escapes** during the fight; his son's defeat buys the time. Quy Nguyên's work continues because of it. **[new]**
- **Thủ Mạch Nhân's words over the defeated protagonist:** *"Ngươi vừa quyết định thay cả thế gian rằng ông ta được sống."* (You just decided, on behalf of the whole world, that he gets to live.) This is the protagonist's **first decision on behalf of everyone**, made from love, not principle. **[new; NB core question]**

### T4. Why the protagonist is spared

- **He is not an executioner.** His oath covers only what the seal requires, and a man defending his father is not a threat to the seal. **[new]**
- **He recognizes the fragment.** He senses the Thanh Mộc Mạch bonded in the protagonist's chest **[FB F2–F3]** and the Mạch Ấn he has absorbed. He has seen this once before, in the age of the Nine, and he wants to know what the protagonist will become. **[new]**
- **The irony is deliberate:** sparing him is also a decision made on someone's behalf. Thủ Mạch Nhân decides that this man is allowed to keep choosing. **[new; NB theme]**

### T5. Power-ceiling fight design (Ch. V)

| Phase | Design |
|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | A normal, fair, beatable boss: health bar, readable patterns, a sparse sword-and-seal moveset. The player wins. **[NB]** |
| **Transition** | He stands up. His health bar does not refill; it **dissolves** into seal glyphs. The UI itself shows that the rules have changed. **[new]** |
| **Phase 2: Sealing** | His power is **sealing**. One by one, he locks away the player's tools: first equipped Mạch Ấn, then class abilities (one counter per class), then stamina regeneration. The player's build is dismantled in front of them. **[new; GB]** |
| **Class counters** | **Kiếm Khách:** never attacks into a parry; he waits. **Ảnh Vệ:** area attacks with no dodge window. **Ngự Linh:** summons and spells are sealed on cast. **Cơ Sư:** traps and devices are deactivated as they are placed. **[new; GB classes]** |
| **End** | When the player's health reaches zero, or after a time limit, a cutscene plays. **No Game Over.** All sealed tools are restored afterwards. **[NB]** |

- **Fairness rule:** Phase 2 must stay readable. The player can survive for a while and feel skilled doing it. The loss must come from the ceiling, never from cheap or unreadable damage. **[new]**
- **Mechanical motif:** his tools are the ancestors of the Người Dẫn Mạch's copper rods. The village craft descends from the guardian's instruments, and the Father's rods are a crude echo of his. **[new; Ch1]**

### T6. Visual identity

- Very tall and gaunt. He wears archaic armour and robes in a style from before Đại Phân Ly. **[new]**
- **Concept pick B, "Bare-headed, white hair":** no helmet or hood; **long white hair** falling behind; a small grey crest; steel-grey archaic armour with a short dark cape; pale eyes. His face is visible, a warrior from the age of the Nine, not a faceless guardian. **[concept v0.1]**
- **Nine seal marks** run across his body, one for each Mạch. Some are cracked and dim, because Linh Suy is visible on him (T1). **[new]**
- **Weapon:** a long, plain blade and a set of ancient seal rods. **[new]**
- **Silhouette:** stillness. He barely moves until he strikes, the opposite of the restless protagonist. **[new; PB L3]**

### T7. Reappearances

| Chapter | Role |
|---|---|
| Ch. V | The scripted loss (T3–T5). |
| Ch. VI — Hàn Sơn | Not met in person. The frozen battlefields hold traces of him in the age of the Nine. The protagonist realizes who spared him. **[new; NB Ch.VI]** |
| Ch. X — Thiên Khuyết | He meets the protagonist without a fight. He admits he never knew *why* the Nine did what they did, only that he promised. He learns the original purpose alongside the protagonist. **[new; NB Ch.X]** |
| Ch. XI–XII | His seal is failing and he is dying. He commits to a final stance according to world state. |

- **No conventional rematch on the critical path.** A rematch would undo the lesson of Ch. V. **[new]**
- **Branch-only late confrontation:** in the Pride and Gluttony branches the protagonist can now defeat him. **Winning is staged as the disturbing part**: the player has become the power the Ch. V loss warned against. **[new]**

### T8. Fates by ending (design guidance)

| Ending | His fate |
|---|---|
| Nhân Hoàng (Pride) | Defeated in a branch confrontation. He kneels to no one and dies, still keeping the seal. |
| Kẻ Giữ Mạch (Greed) | Replaced. The protagonist becomes the new Thủ Mạch Nhân, keeping a promise *he* chose. |
| Ngày Không Còn Thần (Wrath) | Dies when the system is destroyed, released from a promise he never made. |
| Kẻ Thay Thế (Envy) | Dies opposing the Hội, which the protagonist now leads in Diệp's place. |
| Vĩnh Sinh (Desire) | Warns the protagonist against the resurrection, and the warning is ignored. His seal marks go dark one by one as the world's future is spent. |
| Vạn Mạch Chi Thân (Gluttony) | Absorbed. The guardian of the seal becomes part of the hunger that consumes it. |
| Thế Giới Cũ (Sloth) | Keeps the seal, dying slowly, alongside a protagonist who also chose to wait. |
| **Nhân Gian** | **He asks the final question:** *"Ai cho ngươi quyền quyết định rằng không ai được quyền quyết định?"* He is the one character entitled to ask it, having lived inside someone else's decision for centuries. **[new; NB ending VIII]** |
