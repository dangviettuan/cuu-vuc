# ADR-0002 — Nine Realms

Status: Accepted

## Decision

The canonical Nine Realms are:

1. Thanh Mộc
2. Xích Sa
3. Hàn Sơn
4. Vân Hải
5. Lôi Trạch
6. U Minh
7. Huyền Hải
8. Thiên Khuyết
9. Trung Vực

They are not simply nine countries. Each is shaped by a distinct condition of its Linh Mạch and must have a distinct cultural, environmental, gameplay and narrative identity.
