# ADR-0003 — Narrative Philosophy and Ending Structure

Status: Accepted

## Decision

The story will focus on moral ambiguity, contextual decision-making, human limitation, belief and responsibility rather than a binary good-versus-evil framework.

Major choices must produce consequences and may lead to different endings.

There will be seven endings thematically aligned with the Seven Deadly Sins:

- Pride
- Greed
- Wrath
- Envy
- Desire/Lust interpreted broadly as attachment and refusal to let go
- Gluttony
- Sloth

There will also be an eighth ending, **Nhân Gian**, in which the protagonist attempts to move beyond these patterns but discovers that human action cannot fully escape ego and moral fault. The working concept for the remaining fault is **Tội Thứ Tám — Phán Xét**.

At least one required encounter will be unwinnable in narrative terms and designed to demonstrate that neither human strength nor an ideal is absolutely supreme.
