## Notes for the next art pass

- **Five of nine realms chose the darker or dusk mood** (Trung Vực, Lôi Trạch, Hàn Sơn, Huyền Hải, Vân Hải). The game leans twilight overall. **Xích Sa (noon) and Thiên Khuyết (cold daylight) are now the brightest realms.** Keep that contrast deliberate.
- **Trung Vực at lamplit dusk** suits Ch. V (the Biến Loạn). Ch. II, the first visit, may want a slightly earlier hour of the same palette, so that the city's decline reads across the two visits.
- **Lôi Trạch "after the storm"** means storm light should be a *moment* (strikes, Ch. IV's ritual) against a hazy base, not the constant look.
- These sprites are generated. A painted or hand-pixelled pass should keep the **silhouettes, colour families and the reserved red**, and is free to improve everything else.
