# World Bible v0.1

## Origin

The world was once a single continent known as **Nguyên Giới**. Beneath it flowed nine immense channels of spiritual energy called **Cửu Mạch**. They converged at the world's center, **Thiên Tâm**.

The Cửu Mạch sustained life, climate, spiritual energy and the boundary between the material world and the spirit world.

Hundreds of years ago, an event remembered as **Đại Phân Ly** divided Nguyên Giới into nine realms.

Public history describes the event as a catastrophe. The hidden truth is that it was deliberate.

Nine powerful people divided the Cửu Mạch to seal their source: **Nguyên Linh**.

Nguyên Linh is neither god nor demon. It magnifies the will and desire of beings that interact with it. It does not understand moral categories. Healing, domination, revenge and protection are equally valid intentions to it, and every use carries consequences.

The Cửu Vực therefore function not only as geography but as a colossal containment system preventing any single will from controlling Nguyên Linh.

## Linh Suy

After centuries of separation, the Cửu Mạch are deteriorating. This is called **Linh Suy**.

Its symptoms vary by realm: dying ecosystems, mutations, unstable weather, spiritual manifestations, illness and the awakening of ancient beings.

If nothing changes, civilization is expected to decline slowly rather than be destroyed in one sudden apocalypse.

## The Nine Realms

### 1. Thanh Mộc
- Identity: ancient forest, life, symbiosis
- Starting settlement: Làng An Mộc
- Major settlement: Mộc Thành
- Gameplay: poison, root effects, beasts, living environment
- Main early boss: Mộc Giác
- Narrative role: first evidence that a Linh Mạch has been deliberately cut

### 2. Xích Sa
- Identity: desert, heat, ancient buried civilization
- Society: settlements depend on Mạch wells for water
- Gameplay: fire, stamina pressure, environmental hazards
- Conflict: resource allocation and whether a ruler may sacrifice peripheral communities to save a city
- Major figure/boss: Xích Vương

### 3. Hàn Sơn
- Identity: frozen mountain civilization and preserved ancient battlefields
- Gameplay: frost, slow, armor and attrition
- Major boss: Bạch Tướng
- Narrative role: discovery that Đại Phân Ly was intentional and morally compromised

### 4. Vân Hải
- Identity: floating landmasses, cloud sea, suspended settlements
- Gameplay: verticality, aerial enemies, wind currents, mobility
- Major boss: Thiên Điểu
- Potential progression unlock: Air Dash
- Narrative role: ideological confrontation with the Người Đồng Hành

### 5. Lôi Trạch
- Identity: storm-filled wetlands and conductive terrain
- Gameplay: lightning, status chaining, environmental combat
- Major boss: Lôi Nha
- Narrative role: proves that Quy Nguyên Hội did not create Linh Suy, though they are accelerating change

### 6. U Minh
- Identity: thin boundary between the living world and memories of the dead
- Gameplay: spirits, illusion, curse, unreliable perception
- Major boss: Vô Diện
- Narrative role: protagonist's internal collapse and discovery of the true origin of their Mạch Ấn ability

### 7. Huyền Hải
- Identity: islands, coastal cities, tidal ruins, ancient ocean beings
- Gameplay: water interaction, maritime hazards, ancient beasts
- Major boss: Huyền Kình
- Narrative role: definitive evidence that the Cửu Mạch genuinely are dying

### 8. Thiên Khuyết
- Identity: ruins of a technologically advanced pre-Đại Phân Ly civilization
- Visual language: ancient magitech rather than neon science fiction
- Gameplay: constructs, mechanisms, ancient facilities
- Major boss: Thiên Cơ Tướng
- Narrative role: reveals the original purpose of the seal and that Nguyên Linh is not inherently evil

### 9. Trung Vực
- Identity: humanity rather than a natural element
- Major city: Thiên Kinh
- Role: politics, religion, trade, schools, factional power, world-level hub
- Story behavior: initially prosperous, later unstable, eventually a center of systemic crisis

## Quy Nguyên Hội

A secret organization researching Linh Suy over generations.

Their conclusion is that maintaining the current system guarantees eventual collapse. They seek to reconnect the Cửu Mạch.

This may heal the world, but will also break the seal around Nguyên Linh.

Core belief:

> **Một thế giới chắc chắn sẽ chết không thể được bảo vệ chỉ vì con người sợ đánh cược.**

They are not the canonical villains. Their actions can be cruel, but important parts of their diagnosis are correct.
