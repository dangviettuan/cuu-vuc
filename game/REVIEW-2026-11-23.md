# Cửu Vực — Early-State Review (greybox @ `ec3427b`)

**Date:** 2026-11-23. **Scope:** the playable greybox (`game/`), compared with the design canon (`docs/`) and the roadmap's next milestone, the **vertical slice (Prologue + Ch. I)**.
**Method:**
- Read all of `game/src` (about 2.8k lines), `PLAYTEST_REPORT.md` (sections A–H), the Combat & Class Bible, the Prologue spec and ADR-0008.
- Ran `npm run build`: clean.
- Ran a DEBUG-ASSISTED probe (forced deaths in the arena, a 1.5 s main-thread stall) to confirm the suspected bugs.

This review does not repeat what `PLAYTEST_REPORT.md` already covers. It records what is **still open**, what is **new**, and what has to exist **before content production**.

---

## 1. Verdict

**The feel layer is ahead of everything around it.** Input latency, the frame-tagged animator, simulation-time hit-stop, pooled effects and the playtest harness are all good engineering.

The slice is still a **combat toy in a corridor**:
- it has none of the systems the vertical slice needs (save, dialogue, stage data, menus, gamepad);
- the story pillar has not been tested in the playable at all;
- every tuning number so far comes from a bot that reacts in 10–30 ms.

**Recommendation.** Build foundations, not content, next:
1. Fix two confirmed bugs (hours; **done**, §2).
2. Make three design decisions that every later asset depends on.
3. Build the systems layer (about 1–2 weeks).
4. Get real human players on it.
5. Only then start Prologue S2–S8 and Ch. I.

## 2. Confirmed bugs (new, not in the playtest report)

| # | Bug | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | **The arena colliders leak.** `engage()` registers 2 new colliders (player↔walls, boss↔walls) on every boss attempt and never removes them. | Probe: the active collider count goes 5 → 7 → 9 → 11 → 13 over 4 deaths. | Register both colliders once in `create()` (the `walls` group already exists there). `scenes/Prologue.ts:366-367` |
| **B2** | **Herbs never refill, so a struggling player spirals downward.** Respawn and the shrine restore HP only. Once the 2 herbs are spent, every later boss attempt starts with 0 herbs, and the player who is losing gets the hardest fight. | Probe: 0 herbs after each respawn. `player.ts:101-108`, `Prologue.ts:345-361`, `Prologue.ts:466-471` | Decide the herb model (§5, D4). Recommended: herbs are a **flask that refills at the shrine and on respawn** (2 charges in the Prologue). |

**Status: both fixed** (in the same commit as this review, after it was written).
- **B1:** the colliders are registered once in `create()`.
- **B2 (interim):** respawning and resting at the shrine refill herbs to the number found so far (`Player.herbMax`). D4 still decides the final healing model.

**Re-test:** the probe now shows a flat 7 colliders and 2 herbs after every respawn. `03-full-run.cjs` passes every step: a death with the boss on 4 HP, then a clear on the second attempt, which started with 2 herbs (it would have had 0 before). There were no errors, p95 frame time was 16.8 ms, and the object count stayed flat at 360.

**Checked and fine:** a 1.5 s main-thread stall moved simulation time by only 117 ms, because Phaser clamps the frame delta. Timers do not jump after a hitch or a background tab.

**Risk to verify:** Arcade physics runs at a fixed 60 Hz step (`fixedStep: true` by default), while `update()`, the animators and `separate()` run every render frame. On 120/144 Hz monitors, sprites may judder, or code may read `blocked.down` from a stale physics step. Test on a high-refresh display. If it judders, either cap rendering at 60 fps or set `physics.arcade.fps` to match.

## 3. Combat and design gaps (new findings)

1. **Thế does nothing.** Every successful parry grants +1 Thế, and a riposte costs 1, so a riposte is always available after a parry. Banked pips (up to 3) have no use. The HUD shows a resource that never forces a decision. *Fix:* spend 2–3 Thế on **Phá Thế** (the Bible's guard break), or scale riposte damage and poise by pips spent. `player.ts:156-158, 314-315`
2. **A perfect dodge has no effect.** It shows a toast ("Né hoàn hảo!") and nothing else. That is correct for Kiếm Khách by the Bible, but the toast promises a reward. Remove the toast for classes that don't use perfect dodges, or give a small universal reward, such as a stamina refund. `player.ts:306`
3. **There is no block.** The Bible says blocking drains stamina and that Kiếm Khách carries an off-hand guard. The prototype only has parry. Decide whether holding I is a block (chip damage, stamina drain) and a tap is a parry. This changes how every boss is tuned.
4. **Stamina rarely matters.** It regenerates at 32/s, and parries refund their cost. Mashing never dropped stamina below 47 (PLAYTEST_REPORT B3). It only bites in the boss fight.
5. **The boss AI is a weighted coin flip.** `choose()` uses `Math.random()` with no anti-repeat, no memory and no reaction to the player healing or hugging her (`enemies.ts:346-352`).
   - Add an anti-repeat rule (no move 3 times in a row).
   - Punish healing inside 120 px.
   - Add a **seeded RNG**, so playtest runs are reproducible.
6. **Enemy numbers live inside the AI code, not in `tuning.ts`.** Examples are the damage values 15, 22 and 14, the speeds and the recovery tables. That blocks the Bible's **Story / Standard / Hard** modes (§10). Move them into per-enemy tuning tables with difficulty multipliers.
7. **There is no air attack.** Attacks are disabled in the air as a stopgap (I-10). Players expect an air slash in a side-scroller. It needs one clip and a hitbox.
8. **The swipe parry is only proven for a bot** (I-13 is still open). The window works when pressed 0–60 ms before the hit and fails at 180 ms early, and humans press early. Test it with humans before changing the numbers.

## 4. Presentation and readability

- **The hero is still too small (I-08, open), and it's the biggest visual problem.** In `h00-arena-grove.png` and `e02-overlap.png`, all the action sits in the **bottom ~20% of the frame**, and about 75% is background trunks.
  - Recommended: a gameplay camera zoom of **×1.33–1.5** (480×270 or 427×240 visible), with the ground line framed at about 70% of the screen height.
  - This changes the Art Bible's locked numbers, so it needs an ADR **before any frame is hand-drawn** (D1).
- **Fonts.** Text uses system Verdana rendered at ×3 with LINEAR filtering (`Prologue.ts:204-208`). It looks different on every OS and clashes with the pixel art. Choose a pixel font with full **Vietnamese diacritics** (ă â ê ô ơ ư đ plus 5 tones, stacked) and render it as a bitmap font.
- **Scaling.** `Scale.FIT` (`main.ts:17`) gives non-integer scales on most windows, so pixels shimmer as the camera moves. Offer integer scaling with letterboxing as the default, and FIT as an option.
- **The controls strip is always on screen.** It was a good fix for the playtest. In the slice, teach controls in context and move the full list into the pause menu.
- **There is no music.** The synthesised SFX cover feedback, but a "story first" game with no ambient bed or theme is missing half its tone. Even one ambient loop for the forest and one boss track would change how the slice feels.
- **Accessibility.** Hit-stop, flashes and shake are frequent. Add toggles for reduced screen shake and reduced flashes. They are cheap, and some players need them.

## 5. The missing game shell

None of these exist yet. Every one is needed before the vertical slice:

| System | Why it's needed now |
|---|---|
| **Title, pause (Esc) and options** (volume, shake, flashes, scaling) | Anyone opening the public Pages link lands mid-game with no way to pause. |
| **Gamepad** | A 2D action game is played on a pad. Phaser supports gamepads directly, and the `Controls` interface in `player.ts` already isolates input. |
| **Rebinding** | Keyboard layouts differ, and the controls are J/K/I/L/Shift today. |
| **Touch, or a "needs keyboard" notice** | The README advertises the game online. On a phone, it loads and can't be played. |
| **Name entry** (ADR-0004) | The protagonist's name is player-defined, and it's the first onboarding screen. |
| **Save / continue** | Flags are an ad hoc `localStorage` key (`cuu-vuc-flags`). The slice needs a versioned save: flags matching `flags-registry-v0.1.md`, the checkpoint, the inventory and the class. |
| **Dialogue, cutscenes and choices** | S2–S5 and S8 are mostly dialogue, and C1 and C2 are choices. Today a choice is a text box answered with keys 1/2. |
| **Interaction system** | `E` is hard-wired to one shrine. S5 (search the room, move the bed, lift the board) needs general interactables. |

## 6. Architecture readiness for the vertical slice

**Keep as is:** `Animator` and `clips.ts`, the gameplay/presentation split, `tuning.ts`-driven attack clips, simulation-time hit-stop, the `Fx` pool, the outline pass, and the playtest harness.

**Refactor before adding content:**
1. **`PrologueScene` is a 610-line god scene.** It holds level geometry, tutorial signs, ambience, input, camera, the arena, hit resolution, the HUD, the choice UI and flag storage. Split it into:
   - **`InputService`**: keyboard and gamepad mapped to `Controls`, the press queue, rebinding.
   - **A `HUD` scene** running in parallel, so UI is separate from world cameras and zoom.
   - **Stage data**: Tiled JSON or a small in-house format for geometry, spawns, signs, triggers, checkpoints and arenas, instead of the `platform(300, 270, 96, 10)` calls in code.
   - **`CombatSystem`**: the hit-resolution loop (`Prologue.ts:487-505`) and `separate()`, reusable by every stage.
   - **`ArenaController`**: walls, camera bounds, boss reset.
   - **`SaveService` and `Flags`**.
   - **A `Dialogue` runner** that reads script data, so Vietnamese text lives in **string tables** rather than inline (this also opens the door to an English build for the public repo).
2. **An asset pipeline.** `art.ts` generates every sprite from code (682 lines). Decide the authored pipeline now: **Aseprite → JSON atlas → the same clip keys** (D3). The clip and tag system is already built to swap textures without touching gameplay.
3. **CI is build-only.**
   - `playwright-core` isn't in `devDependencies`, and `harness.cjs:21-28` finds Chromium only under the macOS cache path, so the playtest scripts can't run in CI.
   - Add a PR job that runs the typecheck plus a headless smoke run (`03-full-run.cjs`) on Linux, so regressions in feel and flow are caught automatically.
4. **Doc drift.**
   - `game/README.md`'s title says "Greybox Prototype v0.1", but the content is v0.3.
   - `PROJECT_STATUS.md` still lists "Greybox playtest and tuning" as the next task, though that pass is done.

## 7. Canon and spec gaps the slice will hit

- **A classless Prologue.** The canon is a brush-knife with no class (PB L9). Nanh Rễ was tuned around Kiếm Khách's 12-frame parry and Riposte. In the real Prologue the player has the **universal 8-frame parry and no Riposte**, so the boss needs retuning for that kit, or the canon needs amending (D2).
- **Only S1 and S7 exist.** C2 arrives without the context that gives it weight (S2–S6: the dry well, the Father, the forest turning). The story pillar, the project's first pillar, has had **no playable test**. The cheapest validation is S3 (lunch with the Father) at greybox fidelity, with the dialogue runner.
- **C1 and its consequences** (`PRO_VILLAGE_TOLD`, the S6 village damage) need the S6 village stage, which is the second greybox stage to build.
- **The checkpoint rule** (Bible §10): resting should restore status meters and **respawn enemies**. The shrine currently only heals. That's harmless in this slice, but it matters once Ch. I has repeatable enemies.

---

## 8. The single biggest unknown: no human has played it

Every difficulty and feel judgement comes from a bot with 10–30 ms reactions. The report flags "confirm with a human" more than once. Before any more tuning:
- run **3–5 people** for 10–15 minutes each, on keyboard and on a gamepad;
- give them a 6-question form: deaths, the moment they understood parry, what felt unfair, swipe-parry success, jump feel, whether they'd replay;
- record each screen with the F1 hitboxes off.

| # | Decision | Recommendation |

| **D6** | Block or parry only | Hold I = block (chip damage, stamina drain); tap I = parry. |

### Implementation results (2026-11-25)

**DEBUG-ASSISTED probes** (real key input; teleports for setup only):
- **Block** a boar charge at full stamina: −4 HP chip, −24 stamina, the guard holds.
- **Block at 10 stamina:** the guard breaks, 11 of the 15 damage lands, and the player is knocked into hurt.
- **Phá Thế** with 2 Thế: Thế goes 2 → 0, the boss takes 30 damage and staggers.

**`03-full-run.cjs`** (the bot, real input). Every step passed on every run, with no console errors, p95 frame time of 16.7–16.8 ms and a flat object count:

| Build | Result |
|---|---|
| Prologue kit, boss 320 HP | 0 deaths (115 s); 1 death, then a clear (69 + 207 s) |
| Prologue kit, boss 260 HP | 0 deaths (188 s); 4 deaths, twice with the boss on 4 HP, then a clear |
| Kiếm Khách kit, 260 HP | 0 deaths |
| Canvas renderer | 0 deaths |

The bot's results swing widely, and it never blocks. **The Prologue boss's difficulty is the first question for the human playtest (§8).**

**`ci-smoke.cjs`:** 15/15 passes over 5 rounds × 3 variants (Prologue kit, Kiếm Khách kit, Canvas).
